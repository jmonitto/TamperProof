[Screws 101 illustrated guide, verified October 28 2026] https://www.tamperproof.com/screws-101 — The guide illustrates six head styles and four thread/point types. Pan head: low profile, broad diameter and high-edged crown for strength. Flat head: sits flush in metal or countersinks into softer materials for a clean appearance. Truss head: low profile and large diameter, described as increasing fastening strength and resisting removal by vice-grips. Round head: general-purpose, high crown and deep recess, with a smaller diameter than the guide's truss or pan heads. Oval head: for a countersunk installation with a rounded head visible slightly above the surface. Button head: general-purpose with a lower crown and shallower recess than the round head. Sheet metal: Type A or AB thread-forming screw with a locating point; the illustration cites ANSI/ASME B18.6.4. Metric machine: standard metric thread with a straight sheared point; the guide lists Snake Eyes, Torx and Socket Pin-Head drives. Self-drilling: Type AB thread, 410 heat-treated stainless steel, #2 self-drilling point; the guide identifies a pan-head Torx Pin-Head example and a maximum #14 gauge. Machine MS: standard machine screw thread and a straight sheared point; the illustration cites ANSI/ASME B18.6.3. These describe the illustrated examples, not proof that a particular variant is currently offered or suitable for a job. Ask the company to verify current specifications and fit.
